Idea Pipeline: privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Idea Pipeline is a personal, non-commercial research tool run by its developer on their own computer. It has no users other than its developer.

What it collects

Public video statistics and the text of public comments, read through YouTube API Services (and public posts from Reddit, Hacker News and product review sites that the developer saves by hand). It does not collect or store usernames, channel names, profile information or any other personal data. @mentions are removed from comment text before it is stored.

How it is used

Only to summarise the problems people describe and to count how often each problem appears, in a private report. Numbers in the report are generated independently by Idea Pipeline; they are not YouTube metrics.

How long it is kept

Comment text and statistics from YouTube API Services are deleted after 30 days at most. Only the developer's own summary and a link to the comment remain.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared, published or used for advertising.

Google

Idea Pipeline uses YouTube API Services, under the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke an app's access through Google security settings (Idea Pipeline does not ask for access to any Google account).
